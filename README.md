## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a **mobile engineer with 10+ years of software development experience**, building native iOS and Android apps since 2016. I've delivered **20+ mobile applications**, from independent products to established enterprise platforms.

I work across **Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit, Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and Kotlin Multiplatform**, with a particular interest in architecture, offline-first applications, developer experience and modernization of existing codebases.

> **My approach:** Keep the user experience native. Share business logic where it adds value. Prefer clear, maintainable solutions over unnecessary complexity.

## 💡 How I work

- **Native where it matters:** Preserve platform conventions and user experience.
- **Share deliberately:** Reuse business and data logic without forcing shared UI everywhere.
- **Production first:** Account for migrations, compatibility, observability, accessibility and release stability.
- **Pragmatic collaboration:** Work closely with design, backend, QA and product teams; explain trade-offs clearly.
